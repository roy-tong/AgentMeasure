# AI service charges and delivery: commercial scope

Updated 2026-10-21.

Verovo helps enterprise buyers verify AI service charges and delivery
with evidence they can check. The first validation focus is a specific
outcome-billed AI support task, not four separate product launches.

## Who needs the result

The end customer, the enterprise buying AI support and the AI vendor have
different roles. Customer silence alone does not establish success or failure.
The applicable billing definition and the buyer's business requirements must
be evaluated separately.

Support operations explain the business facts. Finance or procurement owns the
spend and approval process. The vendor must be able to review a claimed billing
difference. A task needs a named decision, usable records and someone who can act.

## Two buyer tasks

| Task | Useful result | Commercial status |
|---|---|---|
| Billing correction | Rule-linked, two-sided findings that can enter vendor review | Exploring a fee tied to actual realized net recovery, for suitable cases |
| Acceptance or renewal | Evidence against agreed business requirements, for a specific decision | Exploring a bounded project fee |

Neither mechanism is a standard package, published rate or recovery promise.
Scope, available capabilities, required input and any fee must be agreed before
paid work. A fit discussion is not a free full-period audit or an accepted order.
The existing $10 Healthcheck and $20 Metering QA services have separate,
[published terms](https://roy-tong.github.io/verovo/services.html).

## A finding must lead somewhere

A billing task proceeds from usable records to supported differences, vendor
review, actual correction and agreed fee collection. Track each separately.
Issued credits may expire; distinguish them from credits actually used. Include
findings in the vendor's favor and do not treat missing evidence as an overcharge.

A procurement task instead needs an upcoming decision, explicit business
criteria and a result the buyer can use. An unsuccessful recovery attempt does
not automatically become a paid procurement report.

Success-based work must cover acquisition, unsuccessful screening, review and
follow-up costs. A corrected error may never recur. Repeat revenue requires a
new task the customer chooses to purchase; monthly billing alone does not
establish a subscription business.

## Providers and partners

A provider may have a concrete reason to commission work: lost billable revenue,
a customer dispute, or a procurement requirement. Generic interest in accurate
metrics is not enough to infer paid demand. A provider checking its upstream
model or cloud bill is the buyer in that transaction.

Procurement advisers, support implementers and spend-management partners can
help with a real client task if they reduce data-access, business-interpretation
or coordination work. Start with a bounded engagement before discussing ongoing
work or integration. Disclose relevant commercial relationships; paying does
not change the finding. Public technical findings are not sales pressure on
the organization being reviewed.

## What stays open, and what is deferred

Rules, counterexamples, adapters and reproducible checks stay open source.
Users may self-serve. Paid work must add something beyond running an available
command or repeating general advice. Contributions and research have value
independently of commercial conversion.

We retain calculation reproduction and evidence review. Full execution replay,
a general hosted quality platform and training-trajectory selection are not
current product commitments. Training suitability requires a separate buyer,
learning objective, data permission and outcome evaluation.

Technical adoption does not prove buyer demand. We separately track a usable
delivery, customer action, vendor acceptance, realized benefit, fee collection
and a new-task repeat purchase.
