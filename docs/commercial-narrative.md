# Buyer-side AI charges and delivery review

Updated 2026-10-31. [Product definition](product-definition.md).

Verovo serves teams accountable for AI spending and results. Business and
engineering owners explain the work; finance and procurement help with spend
and approval. Identify who provides records, uses the conclusion and can
commission work.

## Two tasks with independent purchase reasons

| Task | Result | Commercial hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Charge review | Explain charges, reconcile records, identify supported differences and gaps | Bounded project fee; success-based terms only for a suitable correction case |
| Delivery acceptance or renewal | Compare observable work with explicit buyer requirements, list remediation needs | Independent bounded project fee, even when charges are correct |

Neither is a launched fixed-price package. Scope, inputs, deliverables and any
fee are agreed before commissioned work. Repeated service is tested only when
customers have a new task and actually choose to buy again. A fit discussion
is not an order. Existing sample-based [measurement runs](https://roy-tong.github.io/Verovo/services.html)
remain free; old $10/$20 diagnostics are not current offers.

## Value and cost

Separate findings, vendor recognition, actual refunds, used credits, review
hours, buyer decisions and collected service fees. Do not count a proposed
claim or an issued-but-unused credit as realized recovery. Do not add the same
benefit twice. A useful review can confirm no difference.

Include acquisition, unsuccessful screening, initial mapping, review, follow-up
and maintenance in delivery cost. If every engagement requires substantial new
work, treat it as a service rather than claiming software scalability. A future
renewal opportunity not yet due is neither churn nor proof of retention.

## Provider and technical work

Providers auditing upstream spend are buyers in that transaction. Downstream
provider work needs a concrete customer dispute, revenue discrepancy or
procurement request. State the commissioning party and retain findings in
both directions. Shared technical infrastructure is not proof of two-sided demand.

Open-source rules, adapters, conformance and diagnostics support reproducibility,
technical collaboration and distribution. Adoption, fixes and test results have
value without being counted as enterprise purchases. A broader settlement,
certification or payment network remains a separate future hypothesis.

## Current delivery

The main-preview buyer review combines user-defined criteria, local observations,
separate conclusions and offline reports. Supported recounts and Buyer Ledger
provide specialized calculations. Human review establishes applicable rules and
business criteria. There is no general automated quality verdict or guarantee of
supplier agreement. [Implementation guide](buyer-review.md).
