# What an optional paid review adds

You can run, inspect and modify Verovo yourself. The public cases,
explanations, fixtures and reproduction steps stay free. If they answer your
question, there is no reason to buy the same output from us.

A paid review is a small, agreed piece of human work on your question. It is not
access to a secret scanner or a claim that only we can diagnose a problem.
The value hypothesis is less investigation and review work for you, with an
answer you can check. Willingness to pay and repeat use remain unverified.

| Task | Possible extra work | Use free help instead when |
|---|---|---|
| $10: one local anomaly | Review the supplied evidence for one unresolved decision, distinguish plausible explanations and prioritize a follow-up check | All we can do is repeat counts, paste general advice or tell you a known input is unsupported |
| $20: one metering sample | Review a bounded field mapping and rule applicability; compare a declared metric if supplied; deliver a rerunnable result and limits | The existing pack already answers the question with no additional mapping or interpretation needed |

Both are human-delivered services. The $20 service is not an automatic scanner.
It excludes token/cache semantics, vendor invoices and pricing accuracy. The
$10 service does not guarantee a root cause, patch or savings. Complex custom
integration is outside these fixed scopes.

## Before payment

We agree on your decision, the free baseline, the remaining question, the extra
work and the evidence needed. One concise scope reply should contain all five;
we do not ask you to repeat information already supplied. If the free answer is
sufficient or the remaining work is unsupported, we say so without charging.

A summary can support limited interpretation; it cannot identify a cause that
is absent from the input. We do not sell known missing evidence back to you.
Checkout availability and existing refund terms are on the
[service page](https://roy-tong.github.io/verovo/services.html#terms).

## Deliverables

Every delivery names the agreed question, input/version/window, what the free
baseline already showed, what we additionally checked, evidence and alternatives,
next action and its verification conditions. A QA delivery also includes reviewed
mapping/unsupported fields, supported invariants, the actual command and output,
and the declared-versus-recomputed metric when such a claim exists.

“No finding” or “unprovable” can be a useful result when the agreed investigation
was performed and its effect on your decision is clear. Merely repeating a known
limitation or formatting the same output is not the intended paid deliverable.
One clarification round is included. Corrections to the same delivery do not
become new orders; extra work needs a separately agreed scope.

## What payment does not buy

Payment does not change a verdict. It does not establish certification, guaranteed
vendor acceptance, an entitlement to a refund, or independent proof of production
correctness. Public findings are not a proprietary database merely because we
assembled them. Someone else may be able to perform the same review; choosing us
is a service decision, not a technical necessity.
