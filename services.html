Paid diagnosis

Everything on this site runs free and local — the check-up, the conformance pack, the ledger tools upload nothing. These two services are for the moment you want a human answer about your own data: fixed scope, fixed price, and a stated evidence boundary. No subscription, no sales call.

Where the line is: the free check answers is something off in my logs? — locally, no account, no email, and it stays free forever. It cannot tell you what the finding means in your setup, whether it's a bug or intended semantics, and what to do next — that is a human reading your sanitized summary with the public 110-tool audit corpus as reference, and that is what the $10 buys. You never need the paid part to use the free part.

Data boundary

The free tools run locally and upload nothing — that stays true forever. For a paid diagnosis you choose what leaves your machine: a sanitized export of your own picking, and nothing else. We look at what you attached, we don't re-collect it, and input is used for your delivery only.

Terms, before you pay

  • Full refund any time before delivery starts (we haven't opened your input).
  • Unadaptable sample: refused before payment if you checked fit first; refunded in full if it turned out unadaptable after payment.
  • If we can't deliver within 72 hours of complete input, full refund — yours to keep the report anyway if it arrives.
  • "No bug found" or "not enough evidence" is a delivered result, not grounds for refund by itself — that possibility is priced in, and it's why the evidence boundary is stated in every report.
  • $500+ deep dives (multi-system, custom field mappings) exist on request — they are never required for the two services above.
