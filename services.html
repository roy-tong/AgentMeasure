Metering QA check — $20, one sample

Check whether attempts, operations and reported metrics agree

$20 · one sample, one ruleset, one metering question · human-delivered

  • Who it's for: authors of MCP servers, APIs and agents that report usage — especially after changing retry logic or instrumentation.
  • You send: one event sample in JSONL (up to 10k events) plus the number you currently report, if available. Without that number we check sample consistency only. Send the schema and first ~20 lines first — fit is confirmed before anything is charged; if we can't adapt it, we tell you and you owe nothing.
  • You get: a review of the agreed field mapping and rule applicability, plus a discrepancy or no-finding report where every verdict is bound to the invariant that produced it (PASS / FAIL / UNPROVABLE — same vocabulary as the public claims index), a check you can re-run yourself, and a concrete reproduction when there is a real error. Compare the free conformance baseline (synthetic example) to decide whether you need extra review.
  • Scope: one sample, one ruleset, one metering question. We confirm the supported invariants and required fields before payment. Real session and operation identities are required for retry checks. Core FMT-002 does not verify token/cache semantics, provider invoices or pricing accuracy. Bigger questions are quoted separately.
  • Turnaround: within 24 hours after payment, fit approval and complete input.
What makes a sample suitable?

Use JSONL: one JSON object per line. For operation and retry checks we need real session/task identity, operation identity, attempt sequence and result. Costs and step counts are needed for those respective checks. Do not invent IDs or fill missing values with zero. Missing identity may mean we cannot take the job.
