What we establish first

The question, period or decision date, applicable rule, available record types and who can act. The supported scope and any fee are agreed before paid work.

How the commercial work is being tested

We are testing bounded project fees for charge reviews and for independent delivery or renewal reviews. Suitable correction cases may use terms tied to actual realized recovery. These are not launched standard packages or published rates; repeated service requires a real new task.

A finding, vendor acceptance, a refund, an issued credit, a credit actually used and service-fee collection are different events. We do not guarantee vendor agreement or recovery. Payment does not change the evidence. The existing free measurement runs have separate sample-based scope and do not cover enterprise invoice or business acceptance tasks.

What the review includes

A defined buyer task and window; applicable charge rules and separate business criteria; conclusions with source references; missing evidence and remediation items; and a record of the buyer’s next decision. Review either workflow or both.
